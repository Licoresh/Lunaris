# Demo guide

1. Open `/lunaris` and introduce the lunar south-pole map.
2. Switch between historical sunlight, historical Earth visibility, elevation and slope. Explain what each layer shows and point out its units, averaging period and map resolution.
3. Select among the six terrain regions and open its 3D elevation mesh. Compare measurements between regions. Point out that three are published research coordinates and three use the centers of their NASA terrain-raster footprints.
4. Run the Sun/Earth window simulation. Adjust the time and assumed-obstruction controls and show the PDF export. Describe its output as an illustrative scenario, not a date-specific forecast.
5. Point to the source links and limitations. Research markers are approximate reference points, and Earth visibility does not guarantee a working radio link.
